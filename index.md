InnovationLet Space is the space desk of the InnovationLet network. We cover the rockets that fly, the spacecraft that don't, and the business models that decide which of those happens. No countdown livestreams in text form. Just what launched, what it cost, and what it's for.

## What we cover

- **Launches**: vehicle programs, cadence, reusability, and the failure investigations that reset schedules.
- **Exploration**: crewed and robotic missions, science results, and the budgets behind them.
- **Satellites**: constellations, connectivity, Earth observation, and the orbital traffic problem.
- **Business**: contracts, subsidies, and the economics that make or break a launch company.

## How we report

We follow primary sources first: launch logs, mission updates, filings, and agency documents. When we cite a claim, we link it. When a schedule slips, we report the slip rather than the press release. The desk publishes daily, with a longer brief each week for subscribers.
